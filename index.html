01

Research

Two signature strengths anchor the group. Everything else — study design, methods development, and collaboration — grows out of them.

Pillar One

Multi-Omics Analysis & Integration

How do you combine transcriptomics, epigenomics, and other omics layers — often across studies — into one trustworthy conclusion?

  • Meta-analytic integration of multiple transcriptomic studies
  • Pathway-level integration and concordant signal detection
  • DNA methylation and multi-omics study design

Pillar Two

Single-Cell Analysis

Single-cell RNA sequencing is powerful and noisy. The group builds methods that make it reliable — from planning the experiment to handling dropout.

  • Dropout imputation and denoising for scRNA-seq data
  • Single-cell clustering and cell-type discovery
  • Integrating bulk and single-cell RNA sequencing

Study design & collaboration

A third strength ties the two pillars together: statistical design and power calculation for omics experiments — the RNASeqDesign, MethylSeqDesign, and FastQDesign frameworks help investigators plan studies that can actually answer their questions. At MCW, the group collaborates across Pediatrics, the Cancer Center, and other departments on funded genomics research, and Dr. Lin leads Core A (Administrative + Bioinformatics) for the CAR T Program Project Grant planning.

04

Prospective Students — Join Us

Inquiries from prospective PhD and MA students are welcome. If your background is quantitative and you want to work on omics data that matters to real patients, read on.

Who fits well

  • Background in statistics / biostatistics, mathematics, computer science, bioinformatics, or a related quantitative field
  • Genuine interest in omics data — genomics, transcriptomics, single-cell data
  • Willingness to learn both methodology and collaboration: building methods, and using them with biomedical teams

What students gain

  • Training in multi-omics and single-cell methods — the group's two signature strengths
  • Real collaborative biomedical projects across MCW, not only simulations
  • Conference presentations and a publication record built during the degree
  • A path graduates have taken into industry (AbbVie, Merck) and academic research (MCW)

How to apply

  1. Email Dr. Lin first (encouraged). Send your CV and a brief statement of your research interests to chlin@mcw.edu.

  2. Apply to the MCW Graduate School — Biostatistics PhD or MA in Biostatistics and Data Science program.

  3. Name Dr. Lin as a prospective advisor in your application, so it reaches the group.
